# Kumul
Kumul Business Hub PNG is a digital platform that helps businesses across Papua New Guinea connect, grow, and succeed. It combines a business directory, marketplace, advertising, publishing, and business resources to increase visibility and support entrepreneurship in one trusted online hub.
Kumul Business Hub PNG
Empowering Businesses. Connecting Opportunities. Growing Papua New Guinea.
Kumul Business Hub PNG is a digital business directory, marketplace, advertising, publishing, and business support platform designed to help businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, organizations, and customers across Papua New Guinea connect through one modern online ecosystem.
🌍 Vision
To become Papua New Guinea's leading digital business ecosystem that empowers businesses through innovation, visibility, and digital transformation.
🎯 Mission
Our mission is to help businesses become more visible, accessible, and competitive by providing affordable digital solutions, business listings, advertising opportunities, and valuable business resources.

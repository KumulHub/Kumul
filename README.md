About Kumul Business Hub PNG

Kumul Business Hub PNG is a digital business directory, marketplace and business support platform designed to help businesses, entrepreneurs, startups, organisations and customers across Papua New Guinea connect through one modern digital ecosystem.

The platform brings together business listings, marketplace opportunities, advertising, publishing, digital marketing and business resources to improve business visibility, strengthen market connections and support entrepreneurship.

Our goal is to make businesses more visible, accessible and competitive in an increasingly digital economy.

Vision

To become Papua New Guinea's leading digital business ecosystem, empowering businesses through innovation, visibility, connectivity and digital transformation.

Mission

To provide affordable digital solutions, promotional opportunities and business resources that help businesses grow sustainably while contributing to Papua New Guinea's economic development.

Key Platform Features

- Business Directory: Discover businesses, products and services across Papua New Guinea.
- Online Marketplace: Create opportunities for businesses to showcase and promote their products.
- Advertising and Promotion: Increase brand awareness and business visibility.
- Business News and Publishing: Share business news, stories, articles and industry updates.
- Business Resources: Provide useful information, tools and guidance.
- Startup and SME Promotion: Support emerging businesses and entrepreneurs.
- Company Profiles: Showcase business identities, services and contact information.
- Events and Announcements: Promote business events, activities and opportunities.
- Networking Opportunities: Connect businesses, customers and potential partners.
- Digital Marketing Services: Support business branding, marketing and online promotion.

Who We Serve

Kumul Business Hub PNG welcomes:

- Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)
- Entrepreneurs and startups
- Freelancers and independent service providers
- Retailers and wholesalers
- Corporate businesses
- Government agencies
- Non-Governmental Organisations (NGOs)
- Educational institutions
- Customers and consumers across Papua New Guinea

Objectives

1. Increase the online visibility of Papua New Guinean businesses.
2. Promote entrepreneurship, innovation and business development.
3. Support SMEs through affordable digital solutions.
4. Connect customers with local businesses, products and services.
5. Create advertising and promotional opportunities.
6. Encourage networking, partnerships and economic participation.
7. Contribute to Papua New Guinea's digital transformation.
